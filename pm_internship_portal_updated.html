Eligibility Criteria

  • Age: 21-24 years (as per Government guidelines)
  • Education: Must have completed Class 10, Class 12, ITI certificate, polytechnic diploma, or bachelor's degree (BA, B.Sc, B.Com, BBA, BCA, B.Pharma)
  • Annual Family Income: Must be ₹8 lakhs or below
  • Skills: Interest in Product Management and related fields
  • Location: Any city in India or willing to relocate
  • Documents: Valid ID proof and educational certificates
